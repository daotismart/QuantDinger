ETF options strike charts: BOLL verticals + MA overlays

Live + history slices for:
  - OI distribution
  - GEX Call/Put distribution
  - Net GEX distribution
  - Time-value yield

BOLL / MA5 / MA10 use ETF daily closes from
GET /api/cn-derivatives/history?chart=etf.metrics&days=180&frequency=day
and sit on the numeric strike axis at the exact price (same as Price).

BOLL = SMA20 ± 2σ. BOLL Mid is MA20, so MA20 is not drawn twice.

Distribution MA5 is a line across neighboring strikes
(Net OI / Call·Put GEX totals / Net GEX / each TV yield curve).
